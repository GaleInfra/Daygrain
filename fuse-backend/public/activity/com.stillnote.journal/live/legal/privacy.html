Privacy Policy for Daygrain

Effective date: November 18, 2026. Daygrain is a private, offline sensory field journal for iPhone and iPad (iOS 15 and later). This policy describes how the app handles information on your device. Daygrain does not provide accounts, social features, artificial intelligence analysis, counseling, or server-side journaling.

Data stored on your device

Your sensory notes, emotion labels, fieldbooks, weekly reviews, revisit plans, drafts, and Recently Deleted entries are saved locally under the app container using JSON files protected by iOS file protection. Photo attachments are copies you select through the system PHPicker; Daygrain does not request full Photos library access and does not modify your original camera roll. Resized JPEG copies stay inside the app container.

What we do not collect

Daygrain does not upload journal content, photos, labels, or statistics to our servers. The app includes no third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, crash reporting services tied to your identity, or cloud sync operated by the publisher. Insights and charts are computed entirely from records you created on the device.

Optional device features

If you enable App Lock, Face ID, Touch ID, or your device passcode unlocks the journal through Apple LocalAuthentication. Optional daily reminders use local notifications only. The app-switcher privacy cover is drawn by Daygrain on your device and is not transmitted elsewhere.

Exports and backups

When you choose Export full backup, Export readable journal, or a fieldbook text export, you pick the destination through the system share sheet or document picker. Those files may leave the device under your control. Full JSON backups are not password encrypted by Daygrain. iOS device backups may include app data according to your system settings; that is separate from any service we operate.

Activity pages shown in a web view

Daygrain may present publisher-hosted activity pages inside an in-app browser for account or entitlement flows required by distribution. Those pages follow their own policies when loaded. Routine journaling does not depend on those pages and does not send your notes to them unless you explicitly navigate there.

Children and changes

The app is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children over the network. We may update this policy when the app changes; the in-app copy will be revised accordingly. Questions: use the Support page in Settings or the support document bundled with Daygrain.
