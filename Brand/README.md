# Daygrain visual identity

**Name:** Daygrain  
**Line:** A little of the day, kept.

Five elliptical grains form a bloom around a small golden center, one grain for each sensory category. The mark is drawn natively with `GrainBloom` and supplied as an editable SVG plus complete iPhone/iPad icon assets. The app icon is opaque. The privacy glyph is transparent.

## Icon palette

The grain gradients run from the outer tip toward the center, clockwise from the top:

| Grain | Outer highlight | Inner color |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oat gold | `#FBE5AE` | `#E7AF56` |
| Sage | `#DCE7BA` | `#A0BF8F` |
| Apricot | `#FFDBB7` | `#F1A076` |
| Rose | `#F4D0D5` | `#E29BAE` |
| Lavender | `#E6D7F4` | `#B49AD7` |

The background combines a terracotta-to-plum base (`#B36E51` to `#80526A`) with soft radial washes: apricot `#F5C79F` at the upper left (42% peak opacity), sage `#B9C99D` at the upper right (26%), gold `#E8CA7B` at the lower left (32%), and lavender `#B9A3D1` at the lower right (46%). The SVG, raster icons, and native brand mark share the palette, geometry, and gradient directions. The transparent privacy glyph uses the same grains.

`JournalTone` keeps the colored text, pale fills, and deeper button gradients together. Clay identifies sensory capture; lavender identifies emotion capture and reviews; sage identifies fieldbooks and filing; honey identifies revisits and favorites; rose gives the journal library a complementary accent. Individual fieldbook and label colors remain user choices.

Page backgrounds blend paper with apricot, lavender, and sage washes. Cards use restrained color tinting, fine borders, and a soft shadow. Body text stays on pale or dark paper surfaces. Primary buttons use deep gradients with white labels; secondary buttons use matching pale fills, colored labels, and borders. Pressed and disabled states are explicit. Pressed scaling respects Reduce Motion.

Custom-color chips and badges adjust their foreground ink toward the readable end of the current appearance without overwriting saved colors. Selected chips choose a light or dark foreground based on their fill. Sensory colors have separate light and dark variants. Chart counts, labels, and recorded-value tables accompany colors. The activity chart uses clay for sensory notes and lavender for emotion notes; intensity uses lavender and energy uses sage.

Serif headings give the journal a paper character. Rounded numbers and native controls keep the interface practical. The app remains in English and uses iOS 15-compatible drawing and controls.

The name is a product-design choice for this implementation. Market availability and trademark clearance have not been established. Icon PNGs were visually inspected; the app UI was not run or rendered as part of this delivery.
